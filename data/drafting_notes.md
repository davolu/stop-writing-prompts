# How the gold replies were drafted

The 60 gold replies are synthetic. A model drafted each one from the notes below,
and then we edited them by hand until they were replies we would actually send.
The optimizer never sees these notes. It only sees the rubric and the gold chats.

House style of the Maple & Co. support lead:
- Start with "Hi <first name>," when we know the name.
- One short, specific empathy sentence. Never "We apologize for any inconvenience" or "valued customer".
- Then the one policy fact that matters, and the exact next step for the customer.
- Under 90 words. No bullet lists.
- Never promise anything the policy does not allow. For exception requests, say kindly that you can't change the rule, offer what the policy does allow, and pass it to a team lead who replies within 1 business day.
- Sign off: "Warmly, Maple & Co. Support"
