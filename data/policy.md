# Maple & Co. support policy

Maple & Co. is an online home-goods shop.

1. Shipping. Orders ship within 2 business days. Standard delivery takes 3 to 7 business days after shipping. A tracking link is emailed when the order ships.
2. Late or lost orders. If an order has not arrived 10 business days after it was placed, support opens a trace with the carrier. If the carrier confirms it is lost, we ship a free replacement or give a full refund, whichever the customer prefers.
3. Damaged items. Report damage within 30 days of delivery with a photo. The customer chooses a free replacement or a full refund. They do not need to send the broken item back. Refunds reach the original payment method in 5 to 10 business days.
4. Returns. Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Return shipping is free for damaged or wrong items. For a change of mind, the return label costs $6.95, or the customer can choose store credit with a free label.
5. After 30 days. No refunds. Unused items can still be returned for store credit up to 60 days after delivery.
6. Exchanges. Free exchange for a different size or colour within 30 days of delivery, while stock lasts.
7. Changes and cancellations. An order can be changed or cancelled within 2 hours of being placed, before it is packed. After that, it must be returned once it arrives.
8. What support cannot do. Support cannot promise same-day refunds, waive these rules, or give discount codes. Requests for exceptions, and complaints about staff, go to a team lead, who replies within 1 business day.
